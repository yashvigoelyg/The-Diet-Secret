## Brand & Style

This design system embodies modern clinical dietetics fused with organic wellness—an aesthetic crafted for a premier Indian nutrition-tech platform. The tone balances scientific rigor with natural vitality: authoritative yet deeply empathetic, quiet, and refined.

The visual style is rooted in **Modern Clinical Minimalism** enriched with organic warmth. Instead of cold hospital whites or hyper-vibrant gym-tech neon, the system leverages muted sage undertones, restorative forest greens, and calming warm cream canvases. 

Key principles:
- **Clinical Serenity:** Spacious, decluttered surfaces that reduce anxiety surrounding nutrition, diet tracking, and metabolic health.
- **Biophilic Precision:** Earthy botanical tones framed with razor-sharp micro-data typography and structured analytical layouts.
- **Understated Premium:** High-touch warmth achieved via soft pill geometry, tactile badge accents, and layered ambient lighting rather than heavy flat shadows or harsh black borders.

## Colors

The palette balances restorative botanical greens with warm editorial surfaces and grounding slate typography.

- **Primary (`#2D4A3E` - Deep Forest Green):** Anchor color for primary interactive buttons, prominent headings, key branding surfaces, and high-emphasis states. Represents scientific stability and botanical depth. Deep variant `#1F382E` serves as active states and high-contrast navigational frames.
- **Secondary (`#588157` - Herbaceous Sage Green):** Used for clinical progress rings, secondary actions, active tabs, and positive nutritional biomarkers. Light sage `#8BA888` is applied for subtle tinted container backgrounds, pill badge surfaces, and focus rings.
- **Tertiary (`#C87D55` - Warm Terracotta):** Reserved for elite tier indicators, metabolic highlight metrics, premium subscription callouts, and warm motivational badges.
- **Neutral (`#1E293B` - Deep Slate):** Primary reading color for all high-contrast typography, paired with `#0F172A` for titles and `#64748B` for secondary clinical labels.
- **Canvas & Containers:** Base application canvas uses soft cream (`#FAF8F5`). Surface cards and raised modules utilize crisp pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle structural borders rendered in warm light beige (`#EAE5DE` and `#F3EFEA`).

## Typography

The type system blends the geometric clarity and humanist warmth of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines with the neutral, hyper-legible precision of **Inter** for clinical records, macro metrics, and long-form dietary guidance.

- **Headlines & Titles:** Set in Plus Jakarta Sans with subtle negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to maintain a clean, editorial presence across desktop and mobile screens.
- **Clinical Data & Metrics:** Set in Inter with tabular figure alignment (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) enabled across all macro breakdowns, calorie counts, glycemic load indices, and lab values.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Rendered with elevated letter spacing and medium/semibold weights to ensure rapid scanning during consultation appointments and daily intake logging.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model utilizes a standard 12-column fluid grid system on desktop (`min-width: 1024px`), an 8-column layout on tablet (`768px - 1023px`), and a 4-column layout on mobile devices (`<768px`).

- **Outer Margins:** Sized at `3rem` (48px) on desktop to establish an airy, clinical-atelier canvas, reducing down to `1.25rem` (20px) on mobile viewports for compact screen efficiency.
- **Column Gutters:** Scaled from `1.5rem` (24px) on desktop to `1rem` (16px) on mobile.
- **Component Rhythm:** Interior padding follows an 8pt spatial baseline:
  - Metric chips & compact inputs: `space-xs` (4px) to `space-sm` (8px).
  - Form elements & standard button bars: `space-md` (16px).
  - Patient charts, diet sheet cards, meal logging slots: `space-lg` (24px).
  - Major dashboard section separations: `space-xl` (40px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through **Tonal Layering** accompanied by **Subtle Low-Contrast Outlines** and **Soft Pill Ambient Shadows**. Harsh drops and high-contrast dark box shadows are strictly avoided to sustain the calm, clinical atmosphere.

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Flat `#FAF8F5` (Soft Cream).
- **Level 1 (Clinical Cards & Data Modules):** Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border of `#EAE5DE`. Elevated by an ambient shadow: `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(45, 74, 62, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Cards & Floating Toolbars):** Pure white with a 1px border of `#EAE5DE`, raised on hover with `0px 10px 28px -4px rgba(45, 74, 62, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs, Diet Consult Drawers & Dropdowns):** Pure white with `0px 20px 40px -8px rgba(31, 56, 46, 0.12)` and a soft ambient backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background-color: rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.8)`).
- **Pill Shadows:** Dedicated micro-shadow for isolated floating badges, floating quick-log pills, and floating action buttons: `0px 3px 12px rgba(45, 74, 62, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The interface employs a balanced rounded geometric language (Level 2). This creates soft, approachable clinical components without falling into excessive playfulness.

- **Standard Containers (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Input text boxes, metric stat units, data table containers, and nutrition chart wrappers.
- **Large Cards & Modules (`1rem` / 16px):** Meal plan overviews, clinical biomarker panels, appointment consultation cards, and modal windows.
- **Pill Elements (`9999px` / Full Radius):** Buttons, macro breakdown chips, status tags (e.g., "Ketogenic", "Fasting", "High Protein"), tab bars, and quick-add actions.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Forest green background (`#2D4A3E`), crisp white text (`#FFFFFF`), full pill radius (`rounded-full`), `px-6 py-3`, with an active state of `#1F382E`. Subtle hover translation: `translate-y-[-1px]` with ambient shadow `0 4px 14px rgba(45, 74, 62, 0.2)`.
- **Secondary:** Soft sage-tinted container (`#8BA888` at 15% opacity), forest green text (`#2D4A3E`), no border, full pill radius. Hover: `#8BA888` at 25% opacity.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background, slate text (`#1E293B`), subtle underline on hover or warm beige background fill (`#F3EFEA`).
- **Premium Plan Action:** Warm terracotta background (`#C87D55`), crisp white text, pill radius, with subtle warm ambient shadow.

### Chips & Pill Badges
- **Clinical Category Chips:** Height of 28px or 32px, full pill radius, padded `px-3 py-1`. Neutral chips use a beige background (`#F3EFEA`) with slate text (`#1E293B`).
- **Nutritional Status Pills:** Sage green background (`rgba(88, 129, 87, 0.12)`), text `#588157`, border `1px solid rgba(88, 129, 87, 0.25)`.
- **Premium Plan Badges:** Terracotta background (`rgba(200, 125, 85, 0.12)`), text `#C87D55`, paired with an optional 12px metallic-inspired star or crown icon.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Fields:** Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#EAE5DE`, 8px corner radius (`rounded-md`), `px-4 py-3`, placeholder text in `#94A3B8`.
- **Focus State:** 1px border `#588157` with an outer 3px glow ring in `rgba(88, 129, 87, 0.15)`. No default black outlines.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Forest green fill (`#2D4A3E`) when checked with pure white check mark or dot. Unchecked state features a 1.5px border in `#CBD5E1` against white backgrounds.

### Cards & Clinical Panels
- **Meal & Metric Cards:** Pure white canvas (`#FFFFFF`), 16px radius (`rounded-lg`), 1px solid border `#EAE5DE`, ambient drop shadow Level 1.
- **Internal Card Dividers:** 1px hairline rule in `#F3EFEA`.
- **Header Treatment:** Eyebrow label in 11px uppercase Inter (`label-sm`), bold title in Plus Jakarta Sans (`headline-sm`).

### Nutrition-Specific Components
- **Progress Rings & Macro Bars:** Base track rendered in `#F3EFEA` (warm light beige). Value bars use rounded stroke caps:
  - Carbohydrates: Sage Green (`#8BA888`)
  - Protein: Forest Green (`#2D4A3E`)
  - Healthy Fats: Warm Terracotta (`#C87D55`)
- **Metric Micro-Counters:** Large numerical readouts in Plus Jakarta Sans (`24px` to `32px`, bold) paired directly below with an 11px slate label in uppercase Inter (`letter-spacing: 0.04em`).
- **Consultation Timelines:** 2px vertical guide rule in `#EAE5DE` anchored by forest-green pill nodes for completed sessions and soft cream pulsing nodes for upcoming appointments.
